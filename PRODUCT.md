# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router, TypeScript) with `output: "export"`, deployed as static files to Cloudflare Pages. The user asked for Next.js so the site stays componentised and agent-maintainable; static export keeps it free to host and fast for SEO.

## Users

International seafood buyers: importers, canneries, food processors, pet-food manufacturers and food-service distributors who search for Indian frozen and pre-cooked seafood suppliers, compare exporters, and make first contact by phone, WhatsApp or email.

## Product Purpose

Marketing and lead-generation website for SEA BEST EXIM PRIVATE LIMITED, a seafood export company. Success: buyers searching for pre-cooked tuna and frozen seafood from India find the company, understand the product range and specifications, and contact the directors.

## Positioning

Director-led: the directors personally own quality decisions and talk to buyers directly. Transparent dealings, consistent specifications, buyer-specific product forms and packaging.

## Operating Context

Primary categories: frozen seafood and pre-cooked tuna. Also pet-food tuna (frozen raw / cooked) and value-added cubes and steaks. Products are sourced and processed through a partner network; the company coordinates sourcing, quality control, cold chain, packaging, documentation and shipment.

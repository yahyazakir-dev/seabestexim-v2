# Surface: Home (app/page.tsx) and product pages

Mode: Persuade. Audience: international seafood buyers. Action: call/WhatsApp/email the directors, or send an enquiry. Proof: the real product list, forms and packing specs, director-led commitments. Constraints: no own-facility or volume claims; reduced-motion fallback.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The catch is the interface. One fish at a time swims through the page on a curved current, each one a product line the buyer can source. Refuses the exporter default of a stock harbour photo under a slogan with a row of equal product cards.

OWN-WORLD: Drenched deep-ocean navy (#02326d family) as ground for the hero and catch sequence; teal #04c9c1 as the single luminous accent (current lines, the active state); cold "ice" white-blue bands for reading sections. Bathymetric contour lines as authored texture. Display: Unbounded (wide, engineered); text: Onest. Fish are authored SVG species illustrations with metallic gradients, never clip-art or photos with backgrounds.

STORY: Arrive under water with a yellowfin landing in view and the offer stated plainly; scroll and the range swims past species by species; then surface into the product lines (frozen, pre-cooked, pet food, value-added), how the cold chain works, the eight commitments, the directors, and a direct-contact close.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Floating white header bar so the logo reads in its true colours; phone number top-right, tappable. Headline "Frozen & pre-cooked seafood, exported from India". A yellowfin tuna swims in along a curve and settles. Primary action: Send an enquiry; secondary: call.

SIGNATURE: Pinned scroll sequence. The current fish exits left-to-right on an arcing path (rotating to the tangent); the next species enters from the left, its name set huge behind it, forms available listed beside.

RISK: SVG fish fidelity. Mitigation: parametric anatomy per species, metallic shading, fin and eye detail; verify at 1440 and 390.

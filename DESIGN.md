# Design system: Sea Best Exim

The site is set under water. Deep-ocean navy grounds carry the hero, the swim sequence, the cold-chain band, the contact close and the footer; cool ice-white bands carry reading sections. Teal is the single luminous accent, taken from the logo's wave.

## Type

- Display: Unbounded Variable, weights 500–700, tracking −0.025 to −0.045em. Headlines, prices of attention, big contact details.
- Text: Onest Variable, 400–650. Body at `--step-0`, ledes at `--step-1`.
- Fluid scale `--step--1` … `--step-5` (clamp-based). Display never exceeds ~6rem; species names behind the fish scale from their longest word so they never clip.

## Shape and depth

- Radius 22px for photos and cards, 12–20px for controls, pills for buttons and chips.
- Shadows are long and soft with negative spread (`0 30px 60px -40px`), never zero-blur.
- Texture: bathymetric contour lines (`<Contours>`) at 8–14% teal on dark grounds.

## Components

- Header: floating white bar (logo in true colours), nav, phone button top-right ("Talk to the directors"), WhatsApp, mobile menu.
- Buttons: `.btn` teal, `.btn--ghost` on dark, `.btn--navy` and `.btn--line` on light.
- Species card `.sp`: navy art tile with the SVG fish, name, Latin name, note, form chips.
- Lists over cards: reasons, glossary, cuts and FAQ use hairline-divided rows rather than card grids.
- Enquiry form: white panel on dark, 50px inputs on ice, focus ring in ocean blue.

## Motion

- One signature moment: the homepage parade (`components/Parade.tsx`). A sticky stage scrubbed by GSAP ScrollTrigger and snapped to each species; the current fish exits left-to-right on a rising arc, the next enters from lower left, rotation keyframed (never auto-rotated) so every fish rests level.
- Ambient: SVG tail sway and pectoral flap inside each fish file, a slow bob, light shafts, rising bubbles.
- Section reveals: opacity + 28px rise + blur, staggered, content visible without JS.
- `prefers-reduced-motion`: the parade becomes a static landing; all reveals and loops stop.

## Fish illustrations

Generated by `scripts/fish/` (parametric body, fins, tail, markings, metallic gradients). Fourteen species in `public/fish/`. Add a species by adding a spec in `scripts/fish/species.ts`.
